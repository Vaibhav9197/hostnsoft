# Embarko for Hackathons

Give every participant **2 months of Embarko Pro, free**, and get a dedicated
Showcase page for your event.

Example: `embarko.ai/showcase/iit-indore-2026`

Participants deploy their projects on Embarko and submit them to Showcase.
Your event gets one permanent page where judges, participants, companies and
the community browse what was built.

## [What do we get?](#what-you-get)

- **2 months of Embarko Pro for every participant**
- **A unique hackathon code**
- **A dedicated Showcase page for your event**
- One place for judges to browse live projects
- A permanent gallery after the event
- Simple deploys from participants' AI agents
- No hosting setup or hosting workshop needed

## [How does it work?](#how-it-works)

1. **Apply.** Send us the event name, college or organisation, event dates,
   expected number of participants, and event website or social link.
2. **We create your event.** You get a hackathon code, such as
   `IITINDORE26`, and a Showcase page, such as
   `embarko.ai/showcase/iit-indore-2026`.
3. **Share the code with participants.** They use it to activate **2 months
   of Embarko Pro, free**.
4. **Participants build and deploy.** They build with the AI agent they
   already use, then ask it:

   ```
   Deploy this to Embarko
   ```

   The project gets a live URL. No signup and no credential needed first.
   The code only activates Pro; it is not needed to deploy.
5. **Projects join your Showcase.** Send participants your event page's
   invite link from the Embarko console. They pick their live app and say
   what it does, and it appears on your event page. Or they paste the page's
   Showcase prompt into their AI agent, which does the same.

Judges and your community get one place to browse everything built at the
event.

**Good to know**

- **An app deployed with no credential lasts 24 hours.** Participants claim
  it with a token once it's worth keeping, and before submitting to
  Showcase.
- **What happens on a deploy:** see the [agent quickstart](agent-quickstart.md).
- **If a build fails:** see [`troubleshoot.md`](troubleshoot.md).

## [How do participants keep Pro free for 1 year?](#keep-embarko-pro-free-for-1-year)

By showing what they built.

1. Post a short **Instagram Reel or YouTube video** showing:
   - what you built
   - who it helps
   - a quick demo
   - how you put it online with Embarko
2. Tag Embarko.
3. Submit the video link.

If approved: **1 year of Embarko Pro, free**. Good projects may also be
featured in Embarko Showcase and shared with the Embarko community.

## [What happens after the event?](#your-hackathon-lives-beyond-the-event)

Your Showcase page stays online. Use it to:

- show winning projects
- share shortlisted projects
- let judges browse live submissions
- showcase your participants
- share the event with companies and communities
- keep projects discoverable after the hackathon
